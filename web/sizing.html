Equations used

Monocopter

TWR = Tmax / (m g) (need ≥ 2.0)
hover throttle ≈ √(m g / Tmax) (thrust ∝ throttle², rough)
vane authority: τ = 2 Fv h

Rocket (coaxial pair, hover)

TWR = Tmax / (m g) (need ≥ 2)
Phover ≈ (m g)3/2 / (√(2 ρ A) · FoM), A = disk area of one 9 in prop, FoM ≈ 0.55 for a coaxial pair
flight time ≈ 0.8 · capacity / (P / V)
