Attitude control: what the gains actually do

How to tune (same on both vehicles)

  1. Set Ki = 0, Kd = 0. Raise Kp until the response just starts to ring, then reduce by 30 %.
  2. Add Kd until the ringing is damped (overshoot < 20 %). Too much Kd amplifies gyro noise and wears servos.
  3. Add a little Ki only to remove steady offset (thrust misalignment, CG error).
  4. Increase latency and actuator lag: watch the stable region shrink. This is why every millisecond of loop delay matters.

Reading the plots

Top: attitude error (deg). The vehicle starts offset by the preset step and a short disturbance hits at 1.5 s. Bottom: actuator position (gimbal angle for the rocket, vane command for the monocopter). Flat tops = saturation.
This is a 1-axis, small-angle model with no aerodynamics, no slosh and no thrust curve. It teaches trends; tune the real vehicle from logged flights.
