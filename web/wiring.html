Wiring rules

  • One ground star point at the flight computer. Never daisy-chain servo ground through the logic board.
  • Servo power rail is separate from the logic rail; join grounds only. In the rocket the servos have their own battery (pack B).
  • Solder or use locking connectors. No bare Dupont jumpers in flight hardware: vibration will unseat them.
  • Twist motor phases and battery leads; keep them away from the IMU and receiver antenna.
  • The remove-before-flight pin/loop breaks the main battery supply: the motors and servos cannot move with it in.
