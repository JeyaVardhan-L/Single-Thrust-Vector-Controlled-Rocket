Why a 2:1 gear stage?

A direct servo-to-gimbal link doubles every pulse-width quantisation step and every bit of servo slop into the thrust vector. The 18T pinion into a 36T sector halves both, and doubles the available torque at the gimbal, in exchange for half the angular speed. With ±7° of gimbal you only need ±14° of servo, which is well inside a standard servo's range.
