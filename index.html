Two phases

An electric rocket that stands on its own thrust.

Phase 1 is a monocopter with four vanes in the prop wash. It proves the flight computer, the attitude filter, the control loop, the servos, the logging and the way you work.

Phase 2 reuses all of it on RK-1: a coaxial pair of brushless motors in a gimbal, a three-plate stack and four legs.

Take-off mass1.06 kg
Thrust to weight2.5
Gimbal range±7°
Control loop500 Hz
