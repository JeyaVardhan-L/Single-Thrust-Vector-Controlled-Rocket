# Printable parts (STL)

Generated by `npm run cad` from [`scripts/cad/`](../scripts/cad/). Every file is a watertight solid in **millimetres**, already in its **print orientation** (z up, lowest face on the bed). Sizes are bounding boxes in that orientation. "g solid" is the mass if the part were printed 100 % solid, an upper bound: at the baseline settings (4 walls, 30 to 40 % infill) a part weighs roughly 55 to 80 % of it. Weigh the printed parts and update the BOM if a line differs by more than 10 % (see [printing guide](printing.md), post-processing step 6).

The smallest bed that fits everything is **220 × 220 mm**. Settings, materials and tolerances: [3D printing guide](printing.md).
